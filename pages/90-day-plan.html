 SEO
Build 25 individual FAQ pages on ylopo.com (one per question)
Each page at /faq/[question-slug]. Structure: (1) question as H1, (2) direct 2–3 sentence text answer above the fold — this is what LLMs and featured snippets pull from, so make it a clean declarative statement, not "it depends…", (3) embedded YouTube video below the answer, (4) full transcript as body text. These pages are the highest-leverage AI SEO and Google SEO asset in the entire sprint. Target: all 25 live by Apr 13.
 SEO
Add FAQPage + VideoObject schema to all 25 FAQ pages
FAQPage schema enables the accordion Q&A feature in Google SERPs (highest-impact schema for lead gen pages). VideoObject schema tells AI crawlers the video title, description, speaker, and what question it answers. Add author markup on each page: "Answered by Barry Jenkins, Realtor-in-Residence" or "Answered by Ge, President & Co-Founder." Validate all schema in Google's Rich Results Test before marking done.

This hub page earns backlinks because it's a genuinely useful resource, not a sales page. Share it with real estate coaches and brokerage owners once live. Target: live by Apr 15.
