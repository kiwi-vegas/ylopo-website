Common Questions

Frequently asked

What's the difference between PPC+ and regular PPC Buy/Sell?
▾
PPC+ layers three things on top of standard PPC Buy/Sell: ~10% lower CPL via AI optimization, vetted live call transfers (our call center filters spam before connecting to you), and bundled Ylopo Voice. PPC Buy/Sell is still a strong standalone — PPC+ is the full stack.
Are my leads exclusive? Can other agents in my market get the same leads?
▾
Yes — your leads are exclusive to you. Ylopo does not resell or share leads across agents in the same market. Every lead generated from your campaigns belongs only to you.
If I cancel, do I keep my leads and data?
▾
Yes. Your leads are your data. If you ever decide to leave Ylopo, you take your leads with you. We don't hold data hostage — your contact list is yours from day one.
Why isn't PPC Seller available in all markets?
▾
PPC Seller targets homeowners searching for home valuations. In some markets, that search volume is too low to run efficiently. Your Ylopo rep can check availability for your market during the demo.
What does "live transfer" mean in PPC+, and how does the vetting work?
▾
When a prospect calls the number in a PPC+ ad, it routes to Ylopo's call center first. Our agents verify it's a real buyer or seller — no spam, no vendors — then connect the call to you live. About 7% of ad traffic converts this way, and they're your highest-motivation prospects.
How does Social Ads pricing compare to Google PPC?
▾
Social Ads run at roughly one-third the CPL of Google PPC. The trade-off is intent — Google captures active searchers, social reaches people earlier in the journey. Most high-producing teams run both to balance cost and volume.
What is LSA and do I need to qualify for it?
▾
LSA earns you a "Google Screened" badge at the top of search results. You pay per lead, not per click, and Google handles a level of quality filtering on their end. There's a background check and license verification process — Ylopo can help you through it.
Does spending more on ads get me more leads?
▾
Generally yes, up to a market ceiling. More spend = more leads until you've captured most available traffic. Your Ylopo rep will look at your market, team size, and capacity to recommend a budget that actually makes sense — not over- or under-buying.
