Why These 25

These questions were identified from two sources: the 2026 Growth Marketing Plan (which flagged the decision stage at 31% coverage) and a live audit of ylopo.com in March 2026. Every question on this list either has no answer on the current site, or has an answer that is too vague to be useful to a prospect.

Who Gets Priority

Barry answers anything about the agent experience — he's the social proof engine and the most relatable voice. Ge answers everything: product, pricing, AI, business terms, contracts, and data ownership. As President and CMO/CPO, he has the full picture. When in doubt, lead with Barry on YouTube and Ge on the website.

Where to Start

The 8 Critical questions (#1, 2, 3, 4, 11, 12, 13, 24, 25) will have the biggest impact on demo conversions within 30 days. The three pages that don't exist yet and should: /pricing with real numbers, /compare, and /how-it-works. YouTube is secondary but powerful for questions #6, 9, 10, 13, and 20.
