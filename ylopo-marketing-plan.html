AI Search Optimization
Winning AI Search: ChatGPT, Perplexity & Google AI
How LLMs Choose What to Cite
  • Clear, direct answers to specific questions (not marketing language)
  • FAQ-structured content with question-formatted H2 headings
  • Schema markup: FAQPage, Product, Organization, HowTo
  • High domain authority paired with dense citation patterns
  • Topical authority clusters — 8+ pages covering one topic deeply
  • "Best [X] for real estate agents" query targeting
Ylopo's LLM Action Plan
  • Build /what-is-ylopo — a direct-answer page for 10 common questions
  • Add FAQPage schema to all product and feature pages
  • Launch topical cluster: "AI real estate marketing" (8+ pages)
  • Target "best lead gen platform for realtors" and similar queries
  • Add HowTo schema to existing workflow and tutorial content
3–5x more AI-cited traffic on pages optimized for LLMs vs. unstructured pages. This is a first-mover window — act before competitors catch up.
